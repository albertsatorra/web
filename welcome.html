About My Work

My specialization is in the application of statistics to the behavioral and social sciences and economics, especially the analysis of structural equation models (SEMs, systems that involve latent variables and simultaneous equations). My work is at the interface between statistics, economics, social and political sciences, and psychology. With an early focus on mathematics and statistics, I transitioned into psychometrics, motivated by the need to improve inferential methods for SEMs. In the early 80s I undertook several years of research development at different institutes in The Netherlands, and from 1985 to 1987, I was a Fulbright Scholar at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)—a campus I continued to visit regularly for many years. I have been cooperating with researchers from multiple institutions and disciplines worldwide. In recent years, I also collaborate with research teams at BI Norwegian Business School and contribute to the annual Norwegian Psychometrics Gathering. Currently, I am Associate Editor of Psychometrika.
